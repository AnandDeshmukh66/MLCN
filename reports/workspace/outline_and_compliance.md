# Institutional checklist and report design
Source: all 11 pages of MINI_PROJECT_REPORT_Guidelines_2025-26 (1).docx.pdf, including visually inspected samples on pp. 7–11.

- A4 portrait, single-sided print design; left binding margin 3.75 cm, other margins 2.5 cm.
- Times New Roman 12 pt; body 1.5 spacing; paragraph separation > normal 18 pt line spacing.
- Single spacing for approval, declaration, abstract, captions, references and quotations.
- Cover title 16 pt bold centred; sample degree/student/institution hierarchy; logo from supplied institutional PDF. Unknown names, branch, department and signatures remain placeholders.
- Certificate wording and four signature positions follow p. 8. Declaration wording and six-position signature grid follow p. 9. Required first-person declaration is the exception to third-person body prose.
- Preliminary order follows sample contents p. 10, overriding later narrative placement of acknowledgement. Collaboration page omitted because no collaboration/certification is verified.
- Preliminary footer lower Roman, centered, starting i on title; body restarts Arabic 1 at Chapter 1, continuous through appendices, references and publications.
- Five narrative chapters; sample Chapter 6 contains Appendix I–VI; Chapter 7 is Literature Cited, followed by candidate publications table from p. 11.
- Abstract 100–200 words; CO–PO mapping unapproved placeholders.
- Real Word heading styles; updateable contents and illustration lists; chapter-based caption fields; table captions above, figure captions below; first references precede objects.
- References in order of first citation, bracketed numbers, authors identified in narrative; all authors listed in bibliography. Underline journal volume where applicable.
- 40–100 actual rendered pages; inspect PDF contact sheets, full-resolution sample pages and page-number transitions.
- Print-stage requirements: guide review/corrections before binding; soft binding, white bond paper ≥70 GSM (library copy ideally acid-free or ≥85 GSM), single-sided. These are handoff requirements, not digitally verifiable.

# Outline
1. Introduction: context, motivation, problem, scope, objectives, contributions, organization.
2. Literature: IDS approaches, flow semantics, boosted trees, datasets, evaluation, explanations, related patents, critical comparison, research gap.
3. Present investigation: evidence method; architecture; module contracts; capture; parsing; bidirectional flows; feature algorithms; model; notebook training; orchestration; generator; receiver; launcher; dashboard; provenance; tests; proposed Gemini XAI.
4. Results: provenance, setup, metrics, confusion and errors, ROC/PR, imbalance and baselines, notebook limitations, feature repair, saved live results, tests, runtime and threats to validity.
5. Conclusions: supported findings, achievement matrix, limitations, sequenced future work.
6. Appendix I–VI: full feature schema; module/file inventory; configuration; reproducibility; result integrity; supplementary evidence.
7. References / Literature Cited; patent verification scope; separate publications table.
